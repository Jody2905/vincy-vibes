Tourism-approved taxis

Every driver on the official SVG Tourism Authority list for St. Vincent and Bequia. Search by name and tap a number to call.

Agree on the fare before you set off, and ask whether it's in EC$ or US$. On other islands, ask your hotel or look for taxis at the jetty (on Union Island, near the Clifton market).

    Source: Tourism Approved Taxi Operators, SVG Tourism Authority, checked October 2026. Numbers are shown as published.
