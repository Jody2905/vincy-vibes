Island life

Volcano mornings, lagoon afternoons, soca nights

St. Vincent and the Grenadines runs from rainforest peaks to tiny sandbar cays. Take it slow, and say good morning to everybody.

Beaches and reefs

Swim with turtles in the Tobago Cays, snorkel Canouan's barrier reef, dive the "Critter Capital of the Caribbean".

Waterfalls, the Vermont Nature Trail and its parrots, and La Soufrière rising over it all.

Carnival and culture

Vincy Mas, Nine Mornings, Garifuna heritage and independence since 1979.

Festivals all year

Taste of Vincy

Eat like you're home

  • Roasted breadfruit and fried jackfish

    The national dish. Breadfruit charred over an open fire, with jackfish marinated in lime, garlic and herbs and fried crisp. Breadfruit arrived with Captain Bligh in 1793 and never left.

  • Callaloo soup

    Thick and velvety, made with dasheen leaves, coconut milk, okra and ground provisions.

  • Madongo dumplings

    Chewy dumplings of local arrowroot, coconut, brown sugar and nutmeg.

  • Banana fritters

    Fried banana batter with nutmeg, crisp outside and soft inside.

  • Hairoun and Sunset

    Hairoun, the local lager from St. Vincent Brewery, and Sunset rum from St. Vincent Distillers, including the famously strong overproof.

Did you know?

Rebuilding after Hurricane Beryl. The July 2024 hurricane badly damaged Union Island, Canouan, Mayreau and the southern private islands. Most are welcoming visitors again, but check that your hotel, ferry and tours are running before you book.

Good to know

Money
Eastern Caribbean dollar (EC$). US$1 is fixed at EC$2.70.
Driving
On the left. You need a local permit to rent a car.
Time
UTC-4 all year, no daylight saving.
Emergency
Dial 911 or 999 for police, fire and coast guard.
