Getting here

Most visitors fly into Argyle International on St. Vincent, then hop to the Grenadines by small plane or ferry.

Argyle International (SVD)

Opened in February 2017, about 5 miles from Kingstown. It has a 9,000 ft runway and is one of the Caribbean's solar-powered airports.

From the airport, take a tourism-approved taxi to Kingstown or the south-coast hotels.

The Grenadines airports

  • J.F. Mitchell Airport, Bequia (BQU)
  • Mustique Airport (MQS)
  • Canouan Airport (CIW)
  • Union Island Airport (UNI)

Entry requirements

  • A valid passport and a return or onward ticket.
  • On arrival you'll be asked why you're visiting and where you're staying.
  • Visitors from CARICOM countries, the UK, the Schengen area and the USA usually get up to 6 months; most others 3 months.
  • OECS citizens can stay indefinitely.
  • Some nationalities need a visa in advance. Check the list at discoversvg.com.
  • Extensions cost EC$75 (US$28) per person per month, at the Immigration office in Kingstown or the ports in Bequia, Mustique, Canouan and Union Island.

Practical info

  • Money: Eastern Caribbean dollar (EC$), fixed at EC$2.70 to US$1.
  • Driving: on the left.
  • Time: UTC-4 all year.
  • Emergency: 911 or 999 for police, fire and coast guard.
  • Medical help: Kingstown (784) 456-1955, Bequia (784) 458-3294.
